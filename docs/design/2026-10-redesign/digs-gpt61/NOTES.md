# Digs

Digs rests at the far end of the existing sidebar footer, beside Live. At 56 × 42 pixels, his long muzzle, floppy ear and lean silhouette use the page’s secondary ink. The outline renders at 1.75 pixels.

Preview with `?state=quiet|running|missed|failed`: curled asleep, sitting, head tilted, or quietly listening. The default is failed, matching the source page’s Agent sync failure. These switches change only Digs; the dashboard remains untouched. Add `&theme=light|dark` to preview either theme.

Click Digs or press **d** to call him. He listens and gives one slow tail wag, then returns to his original pose. Reduced motion keeps the pose change and removes the wag. No sound, recurring motion, or extra status messaging. The existing mobile layout hides the sidebar footer, including Digs.
